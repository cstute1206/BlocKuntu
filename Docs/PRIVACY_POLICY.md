# BlocKuntu Browser Extension Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 3, 2026

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the BlocKuntu browser extensions for Google Chrome, Chromium, Brave, Opera, Microsoft Edge, Vivaldi, Mozilla Firefox, LibreWolf, and Waterfox (the **Extensions**) and their connection to
the locally installed BlocKuntu application and service (together, **BlocKuntu**).

BlocKuntu is a local website- and application-blocking tool. The Extensions use the browser permissions needed to apply the website rules, schedules, and time  
allowances that the user has configured in BlocKuntu.

This policy does not cover the independent privacy practices of websites that you visit, your browser, browser-extension stores, or services that you choose  
to contact outside BlocKuntu.

## 3. Data BlocKuntu processes

BlocKuntu does not provide a cloud account or an online browsing-history service. Its browser-related processing takes place on the device on which you installed it. To provide its blocking and time-allowance features, the Extensions process the following information locally:

- **Top-level HTTP and HTTPS URLs.** When a page is opened or its history state changes, the Extension sends the page URL and a timestamp to the local BlocKuntu service so that it can determine whether the page should be allowed, blocked, or counted against a configured allowance. A URL can itself contain information in its path or query parameters; users should avoid placing sensitive information in URLs where possible.
- **Allowance visit information.** If a website is subject to a configured time allowance, BlocKuntu locally records the URL, a local browser-tab identifier, and visit start, activity, and end times. This is used to calculate the allowance.
- **Blocked-navigation events.** If BlocKuntu blocks a page, the local service may retain the URL, the reason for the block, and the time of the event in its  
local event data.
- **Extension health information.** The Extension sends its browser type, extension identifier, extension version, and a timestamp to the local BlocKuntu service. This heartbeat lets BlocKuntu determine whether the local browser integration is available.
- **Locally configured rules and settings.** The BlocKuntu application stores the website and application rules, schedules, allowances and related settings configured by the user. The Extension itself stores one local setting indicating whether the integration has been put into a deliberate stand-down state during removal of BlocKuntu.

The Extensions do **not** read or collect webpage content, text entered into forms, keystrokes, passwords, authentication tokens, cookies, payment details, downloads, precise location, or the contents of browser network requests and responses. They do not use content scripts to inspect pages.

## 4. Why BlocKuntu uses this information

BlocKuntu uses the local information described above only to:

- enforce website rules, schedules, and temporary unlocks configured by the  
user;
- measure time spent on websites that have a user-configured allowance;
- show a BlocKuntu blocking page when a rule applies; and
- maintain and diagnose the local connection between the Extension and the  
BlocKuntu service.

## 5. Where the information goes

The Extension sends the information described above through the browser's Native Messaging interface to the BlocKuntu native host and local service on the **same device**. It is not sent to a BlocKuntu-operated server, analytics provider, advertiser, data broker, or other third party.

The local BlocKuntu service stores the information it needs in local application data and local event records. The publisher cannot remotely view this data through the Extension or BlocKuntu service.

BlocKuntu does not sell, rent, share for advertising, or use browser activity information for profiling or any purpose unrelated to its blocking and allowance features.

## 6. Chrome Web Store Limited Use disclosure

BlocKuntu's use of browser activity information is limited to the user-facing features described in this policy: enforcing user-configured website restrictions and tracking user-configured time allowances. BlocKuntu does not transfer this information for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or any other unrelated purpose. No person at the publisher receives browser activity information automatically because that information does not leave the device.

## 7. Remote code and third-party services

The Extensions do not download or execute remotely hosted code. Their executable code is packaged with the Extension. They do not use analytics, advertising,  
tracking SDKs or third-party data-collection services.

## 8. Retention and deletion

BlocKuntu has no server-side copy of your browser activity data to retain or delete. Local data remain on your device until you change, remove, or purge  
them.

- Removing the Extension removes its browser-managed local extension storage, subject to your browser's own behaviour, but does not by itself remove local  
BlocKuntu application data.
- A standard removal path can preserve local BlocKuntu configuration and data. Use BlocKuntu's documented data-purge or package-purge process when you want to remove its local configuration, records, and logs.
- The current version does not automatically delete all local allowance-visit and event records after a fixed retention period.

You can also remove or change your BlocKuntu rules and settings through the BlocKuntu application. If you need help identifying or deleting local data,  
contact the publisher.

## 9. Security

Browser activity information is exchanged only between the Extension and the local BlocKuntu components on the same device; it is not transmitted over the  
internet by BlocKuntu. Access to the local application data is subject to the operating system's user and file-access controls. As with other software stored  
on your device, a person with sufficient access to that device or its accounts may be able to access local data.

## 10. Your choices and rights

You choose whether to install the Extension and which BlocKuntu rules, schedules, and allowances to configure. You can disable or remove the Extension  
through your browser and can remove local BlocKuntu data using the data-purge method described above.

Where applicable privacy law grants you rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability, contact the publisher using the details  
in section 2. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant data protection supervisory authority.
